## About

I am an experimental photonics researcher with over nine years of hands-on
experience in advanced optical and wireless communication systems. I received
my Ph.D. from Dublin City University in 2024, focused on next-generation hybrid
optical and wireless systems for converged access networking. I am currently a
Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Trinity College Dublin, directing and delivering
key experimental activities within the Horizon Europe SNS-JU ECO-eNET consortium.
